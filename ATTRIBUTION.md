## Attribution discipline

Contributions that introduce or derive from third-party material must preserve:

1. the upstream project and source location;
2. the affected repository paths;
3. the applicable license or licenses;
4. required notices and retained license files.

Historical attribution documents are archived by the documentation-closure process, but this file remains the current attribution authority.
